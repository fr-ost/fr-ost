## About

I'm Shahriar Ahmed, Co-Founder of [Unique Labs](https://www.uniquelabs.org), based in Rajshahi, Bangladesh. I finished a B.Sc. in Biochemistry & Molecular Biology at the University of Rajshahi, and I'm now doing a Master's there.

I build Chrome extensions, PWAs, Python bots and automation, and AI tools. I'm exploring Web3 and blockchain technologies, and I like building practical tools.

[shahriarahmed.net](https://shahriarahmed.net) · [X / @0x_nation](https://x.com/0x_nation) · [Unique Labs](https://www.uniquelabs.org)
